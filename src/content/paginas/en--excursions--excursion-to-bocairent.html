Description

What to see in Bocairent: an excursion to medieval magic

Just an hour from Valencia, Bocairent awaits you with its medieval essence, its mysterious caves and cobbled streets full of history. If you want to discover a picturesque Valencian town and you're wondering what to see in Bocairent, this tour is the answer: a journey back in time among impressive landscapes, Arab caves and architecture that will leave you speechless, all with an official guide.

Itinerary through Bocairent: nature, history and adventure

At the appointed time, we will pick you up at the point of your choice to embark on this adventure. On the way to Bocairent, we will reveal the secrets of this magical corner of inland Valencia. As we get closer, you will see how the village emerges from the mountain, with its houses perched on the rock and its unmistakable silhouette dominating the landscape.

Ready to explore one of the best kept treasures of the Valencian Community?

The magic route: one of the essential things to see in Bocairent

We will start our adventure with a walk along the magical route, a journey through nature and history that will lead us to discover the essence of Bocairent: a set of cave-windows carved into the rock, from where we will enjoy breathtaking panoramic views.

Afterwards, we will enter one of the most enigmatic corners of the village. Depending on the level of adventure you prefer, you can choose between visiting the Cuevas de los Moros or the Covetes del Colomer.

If we opt for the Cuevas de los Moros, we will explore a labyrinth of interconnected galleries, excavated in a cliff and surrounded by mystery. Its passages and openings continue to baffle historians, and walking through them will take you back centuries.

If, on the other hand, we visit the Covetes del Colomer, we will discover some small artificial cavities that are easy to access, but with an equally fascinating history. This set of caves, less physically demanding, will allow us to immerse ourselves in the past while still marvelling at its uniqueness.

Whichever you choose, adventure is guaranteed - let yourself be surprised!

The old town: a walk through the village carved in the rock
We leave the caves behind to enter the picturesque old town of Bocairent, a network of cobbled streets, passageways and squares that seem to be straight out of a fairy tale.

Before returning, you will have an hour of free time to enjoy local produce or a coffee.

Return to Valencia

After this day discovering what to see in Bocairent, We will return to the starting point in Valencia with the feeling that we have explored a new world unique place.

If you are looking for a different kind of getaway and you want to discover a different rock-hewn and picturesque village, come with us to discover Bocairent.

Book your place for an unforgettable experience!

Frequently Asked Questions

How will I recognise the guide?

You will be able to find the guide at the meeting point with the official guide accreditation. You can ask for Valencia&Go and, in case you can't find him, you can contact us directly by whatsapp to give you precise indications.

  • NH Hotel: the bus stops at the door of the hotel

  • Serranos Towers: wait on the staircase leading up to the towers

  • City of Justice: red bus stop in front of the building

What have I booked?

You have booked an excursion with transport and official local guide to Bocairent.

The experience includes transfer from Valencia, guided tour of the medieval old town and visit to the main points of interest, plus free time.

Cancellation policy

You can cancel free of charge 24 hours before departure. After the deadline or in case of no-show, no refund will be made.

Can I take luggage/baby carriage on the bus?

Yes, as long as it is a cabin bag or small backpack/foldable car. Please let us know to ensure space on the bus.

Is there a minimum number of participants required?

This activity requires a minimum of 6 participants. If we do not reach this number, we will contact you to offer you some alternatives.
